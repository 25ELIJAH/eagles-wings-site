# Photo and story consent form (template)

Use one form per child or adult before any photograph, name, quote or story is published on the website, social media or in reports.
Keep the signed forms safely, in a locked place or a private folder. Never post them online.

For a child under 18, a parent or legal guardian signs. Where possible, also ask the child if they are happy to be photographed, and respect a "no".
