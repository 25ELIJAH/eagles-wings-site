# Information needed from the owner

Send these to the person in charge. Each answer goes into `src/data/site.ts` or `src/data/org.ts`; until then the site simply leaves the line out.

1. Registration status and number (for example "Registered CBO No. ..."). Shown in the footer, Privacy, Terms and FAQ.
2. Data protection (ODPC) registration number, or confirmation that the group is exempt. Needed for the Privacy page.
3. Name of the child protection lead, ideally with a second contact. Needed for the Child Protection page.
4. Town or office address, and office hours if any. Shown on the Contact page.
5. How gifts are used: the percentage for each of the four uses (feeding, mentorship, medical outreach, running costs). Shown on the Give page.
6. Board members, advisers and key volunteers, with their agreement: name, role, a one-line bio and a photo.
7. Real photos of the community, with permission from the people shown, especially children.
8. Where form messages should go (a form service address), so contact and newsletter messages are not lost.
9. Social media page links, partners' names and logos, any annual reports, news and upcoming events.

## Added since: please send these too

10. Giving details, exactly as they should appear: M-Pesa Paybill business number and account name, and/or Buy Goods till number, and/or bank name, account name, account number and branch. Without them the Give page asks people to message or call.
11. A free Formspree (or similar) account address, so contact and volunteer messages reach info@eagleswingscommunity.org. Steps are in docs/PRODUCTION.md.
12. Whether to switch on anonymous, cookie-free visit counting (GoatCounter). If yes, create a free account and send the site code.
13. Signed photo consent forms for everyone shown on the website (template: docs/PHOTO-CONSENT-FORM.md).
14. For each program, one real story (with consent): first name, a short quote, a photo.
15. A one-page report of income and spending for the year, as a PDF.

16. A Swahili speaker from the organisation to read the Kiswahili pages (/sw/, /sw/kuhusu/, /sw/programu/, /sw/toa/, /sw/wasiliana/, /sw/shirikiana/) and correct any wording. All Swahili text is in src/lib/i18n.ts.
